    Music - ACE-Step 1.5

    Save and explicitly approve exact lyrics and musical settings before generation. Live generation is disabled unless a dedicated process is authorized. Audio approval is separate and bound to the exact output bytes.

    Put each [section tag] on its own line and sung words below it. Match arrangement names exactly, including case: Verse 1, Verse 2, Chorus 2, and Final Chorus are separate sections. Keep timing, instruments, and performance instructions in the arrangement plan, not sung lines. For an instrumental-only section, put its own tag in the lyrics and describe the instrumental passage in the plan; sung text is not needed. Tags organize the draft; they do not guarantee the generated structure.

    Save draft stores changed lyrics/settings as a new revision. Create revision also labels that changed draft; a name alone does not rename an existing revision. Compare uses saved revisions, not unsaved form edits. Restoring creates a new saved revision and reloads the form; save your edits first if you want to keep them.
